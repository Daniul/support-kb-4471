How to read it

  • One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so some rows come twice.
  • The cause comes from the tenant and the way the report came in, not from intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser; the “tagged as” column shows how often it misses.
  • Subject lines do not separate the problems: the same subjects appear in all three. Merging on subject (decision 0001) is how they became one ticket.
  • The parser fix does not end hint: that swap happens in the form, before the parser.
  • The parser fix does not touch the one-day moves.

Lessons

  • Count by cause, not by subject.
  • A footnote is not a ticket. If the queued fix will not close it, it needs its own.
